Shipping Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This page explains how Weliza delivers your online order.

1. Where we deliver

We deliver across India. Enter your full address and pincode at checkout.

2. Shipping charges

The shipping charge is shown at checkout before you place your order. Some orders may get free shipping above a certain order amount. If so, you will see it in your order summary.

3. Dispatch and delivery time

  • Ready-made items are usually dispatched within 2 to 3 working days.
  • Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 working days after dispatch, depending on your location.
  • Made-to-measure items need extra time for stitching. We will tell you the expected time when we confirm your order.

4. Payment: Cash on Delivery

At the moment we accept Cash on Delivery (COD). You pay the delivery person when your order arrives. A small COD fee may be added, and it is shown at checkout before you place the order.

5. Tracking your order

After you order, you get an order number. Use the Track order page with your order number and phone number to see the status. When your order is shipped, the courier name and tracking number are added there.

6. Delivery problems

  • Please give a correct address and a phone number that is on, so the courier can reach you.
  • If your parcel looks damaged when it arrives, take photos before opening it and contact us within 6 days. See our Return Policy.
  • If your order is late, contact us with your order number and we will check it with the courier.
