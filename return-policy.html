Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

We want you to be happy with your Weliza order. This page explains when you can return an item and how refunds work.

1. Return period: 6 days

You can ask for a return, exchange or refund within 6 days of receiving your order, if the problem is caused by us (see section 2).

2. When we accept a return

  • The item arrived damaged (for example torn, a stitching fault, or a mark or defect that was already there when it was delivered).
  • We sent the wrong size or a different item from the one you ordered.
  • A made-to-measure item was not stitched to the measurements you gave us.

3. When we cannot accept a return

We cannot accept a return or give a refund when the problem is caused by the customer, for example:

  • The item is dirty, stained, smells of perfume or sweat, or has been worn, washed or altered after delivery.
  • The item was damaged after delivery.
  • The size you ordered was wrong, or the measurements you gave us were wrong.
  • You changed your mind after receiving the item.
  • The request is made after the 6 days.

4. How to ask for a return

  1. Contact us within 6 days by WhatsApp, phone or email. Tell us your order number and what is wrong.
  2. Send clear photos of the problem.
  3. We will check and reply. If your request is accepted, we will tell you how to send the item back.

When the problem is our fault, we pay the return shipping.

5. Replacement or refund

If your request is accepted, we will replace or re-stitch the item, or refund you. Because we offer Cash on Delivery, refunds are sent to your bank account or UPI ID, which you give us. We process the refund within 7 working days after we receive and check the returned item.

6. Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order before it is shipped by contacting us with your order number. Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled, but the return rules above still apply.
